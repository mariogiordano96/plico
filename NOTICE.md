## Food composition data — CREA

`assets/data/crea_seed.json`, `assets/data/crea_seed_backup.json` and
`crea_food_composition_tables.csv` are derived from the *Tabelle di composizione degli alimenti*
published by **CREA – Centro di ricerca Alimenti e Nutrizione** (https://www.alimentinutrizione.it,
https://www.crea.gov.it/alimenti-e-nutrizione).

Original source: *Tabelle di composizione degli alimenti, Aggiornamento 2019* — website by L. Marletta and E. Camilli.

CREA's site states that the data may be freely consulted and, if used for other purposes (scientific,
educational, commercial…), asks to respect intellectual property rights and to cite the original source.
No formal open license is attached to the data. The data is **not** covered by Plico's GPL license; if you
reuse it, cite CREA as the source and check their current terms. Food images are not redistributed: the app
loads them directly from alimentinutrizione.it.
