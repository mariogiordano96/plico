# Contributing

Plico is a personal project and is maintained on a best-effort basis, with no guaranteed response times.

- **Bug reports and ideas:** open an issue. Include your Android version, the app version and steps to reproduce.
- **Pull requests:** not accepted for now. If you want a change, open an issue first so it can be discussed.
- **Forks:** welcome under the terms of the [GPL-3.0 license](LICENSE): keep the copyright notice, publish your source, and use a different name and icon.
